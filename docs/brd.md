# Business Requirements Document (BRD) — APM Jambi

## 1. Project Title & Goal
**Pengembangan Website Profil & Publikasi Kegiatan Aliansi Perempuan Mandiri (APM) Jambi**

Goal: Establish an authoritative, modern, accessible digital presence for APM Jambi to publish organizational information, program updates, news articles, and activity photo galleries.

## 2. Business Objectives
1. **Public Information Accessibility**: Provide visitors, partners, and community members with transparent information about APM Jambi's mission, history, team structure, and contact points.
2. **Activity & News Publishing**: Enable APM management to quickly create, publish, edit, and archive news articles and activity photo documentations.
3. **Simple & Secure Management**: Offer a lightweight CMS interface requiring zero technical knowledge for daily content creation and updates.

## 3. Out-of-Scope Items
- Online payment/donations
- Automated WhatsApp integration
- Public member registration
- Legal complaint submission form
- Live chat / public forum
